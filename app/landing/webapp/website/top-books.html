Top 10 books in the world

Ten of the best-loved and best-selling books of all time.

Ranked by the sales figures most often quoted for each title. Every one of them has been read by tens of millions of people.

1

Don Quixote

Miguel de Cervantes, 1605

A would-be knight and his loyal squire ride out to fix the world. Often called the first modern novel.

2

A Tale of Two Cities

Charles Dickens, 1859

Love, sacrifice and revolution in London and Paris. Opens with the most quoted first line in English.

3

The Lord of the Rings

J. R. R. Tolkien, 1954

One ring, nine companions and a journey across Middle-earth that defined modern fantasy.

4

The Little Prince

Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, 1943

A small prince visits strange planets and teaches a pilot what really matters. Translated into 500+ languages.

5

Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

J. K. Rowling, 1997

An orphan discovers he is a wizard and starts at Hogwarts. The book that began a global phenomenon.

6

And Then There Were None

Agatha Christie, 1939

Ten strangers, one island, and a rhyme that comes true. The best-selling crime novel ever written.

7

Dream of the Red Chamber

Cao Xueqin, 1791

The rise and fall of a great family, and a pillar of Chinese literature.

8

The Hobbit

J. R. R. Tolkien, 1937

Bilbo Baggins leaves his comfortable hole for a dragon, a treasure and an unexpected adventure.

9

Alice's Adventures in Wonderland

Lewis Carroll, 1865

A rabbit hole, a mad tea party and a Queen of Hearts. Nonsense that has delighted readers for 150 years.

10

The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe

C. S. Lewis, 1950

Four children step through a wardrobe into Narnia, where it is always winter but never Christmas.
