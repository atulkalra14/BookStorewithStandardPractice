Our story

Independent, curious and a little bit old-fashioned, since our very first shelf.

A shop built around readers

Page Turner Books opened with a single table of second-hand paperbacks and a big idea: that every neighbourhood deserves a place where people can browse without hurry, talk about what they read and leave with something they did not expect to find.

Today we stock thousands of titles across fiction, non-fiction, children's books and gift editions, and we still choose every shelf by hand. If we would not recommend it to a friend, it does not make the cut.
